The Rent Hunt

You will be shown 10 flats, one at a time, and your goal is to choose the cheapest one to rent. At each flat you must decide: take it and stop, or let it go and keep looking. Once you let a flat go, you cannot return to it. Each round is a fresh set of flats — play round after round and try to improve your success rate by finding a good strategy.
